# Installing the EFB App on Your Device

## iPad (Safari)

Open the EFB app in Safari, then tap the **Share** button (the square with an upward arrow, located at the top of the screen). Scroll down in the menu that appears and tap **Add to Home Screen**. You can keep the suggested name or type your own, then tap **Add** in the top-right corner. The EFB app icon will now appear on your Home Screen just like any other app — tap it to open EFB in its own full-screen window.

## Desktop (Chrome or Edge)

Open the EFB app in Chrome or Edge. Look for the **Install** icon in the right side of the address bar (it looks like a monitor with a small down-arrow). Click it, then click **Install** in the pop-up that appears. The app will open in its own window and a shortcut will be added to your desktop and Start menu (Windows) or Applications folder (Mac). From then on, just open it the same way you would any other app.
