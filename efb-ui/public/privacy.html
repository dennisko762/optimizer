Crew EFB Platform — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 · Applies to the "Crew EFB Platform" crew application

This policy describes how the Crew EFB Platform ("the App") handles personal data when a pilot signs in via the vAMSYS Pilot API (vAMSYS Pilot API, OAuth 2.0 Authorization Code + PKCE).

1. Data we process

When you authorise the App through vAMSYS, we receive — scoped strictly to the permissions you consent to:

  • Identity (identity:basic): your name and email address.
  • Pilot profile (pilot:read): your pilot profile, rank, statistics and badges within your Virtual Airline.
  • Flights (flights:read): your bookings/roster and the SimBrief OFP linked to each booking.

We additionally store flight plan details you enter in the App (route, weight, fuel, check-in data) locally on your device to support local check-in, boarding and CI/Mach optimization.

2. What we use it for

  • Showing you your pilot identity, rank and current flight roster in the App.
  • Displaying the operational flight plan (OFP) for your booked flights.
  • Local check-in, boarding and cost-index/Mach planning for the selected flight.
  • (Only if you grant flights:write) opening your vAMSYS dispatch / remote check-in link.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect your vAMSYS username or password — sign-in happens on vAMSYS's own consent page.
  • We do not share, sell or transmit your data to any third party (vAMSYS itself excepted, as the data source).
  • We do not store vAMSYS tokens longer than needed for your session; tokens are refreshed automatically and can be revoked at any time from your vAMSYS account.
  • We do not log or display sensitive credential material.

4. Storage

Auth tokens and flight data are held in the App's local, in-memory session store on the machine running the App. Nothing is uploaded to a public server or third-party analytics.

5. Your rights & revocation

You may revoke the App's access to your data at any time in vAMSYS (your pilot settings), or ask your Virtual Airline to deactivate the App's OAuth client. After revocation the App no longer receives your data.

6. Contact

For privacy questions about this App, contact the App's operator (the pilot who registered it with your Virtual Airline). For questions about how vAMSYS handles your data, see vAMSYS's privacy policy.

This App is a non-commercial hobby project and is not affiliated with vAMSYS, Lufthansa, Etihad or Emirates.
